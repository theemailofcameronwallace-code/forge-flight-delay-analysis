## Busiest Flight Times - SQL Findings

# Which day of the week and/or time of the year has the busiest flight days?


# Busiest Months
The busiest months seem to be the summer months (June, July, and August), with the lowest number of flights being in February. This most likely has to do with summer vacations, school hours, and work related trips. 


# Busiest Days of the Week
The busiest flight days seem to be Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday. With the lowest number of flights on Fridays. This might be due to work trips that are more common throughout the year during the weekdays or early weekend vacations.


# Busiest Airlines
Southwest Airlines, Delta Air, and American Airlines are the top 3 airlines with the highest number of flights, with Frontier airlines inc, and Virgin America airlines with the least amount of flights.
